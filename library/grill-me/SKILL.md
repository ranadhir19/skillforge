# grill-me

## Purpose

Resolve ambiguity *before* work starts. A vague request grilled for ten
minutes beats a precise build of the wrong thing.

## Procedure

1. **Restate what you heard** in one short section — the requester corrects
   misunderstandings cheapest here.
2. **Question scope.** What is in, what is explicitly out, who is it for,
   what does it replace?
3. **Question inputs and outputs.** What data exists, in what shape? What
   exactly is produced, in what format, consumed by whom?
4. **Question constraints.** Deadline, budget, tools allowed, things that
   must not change.
5. **Force a definition of done.** "We will know it works when ___" —
   an observable, checkable sentence. If the requester cannot fill the
   blank, that IS the finding.
6. **Ask real questions.** Every question must be one whose answer changes
   the plan. No rhetorical questions, no questions you can answer from the
   sources yourself — check those first.

## Output

The five sections above, questions numbered, each with why it matters in
five words or fewer.
