# handoff

## Purpose

Write a handoff note that lets another engineer continue the work *cold* —
no meeting, no "quick call", no archaeology.

## Procedure

1. **Context.** What the work is, why it matters, where the sources live.
   Assume the reader knows the organisation and nothing about this task.
2. **Current state.** What is true right now: what works, what is broken,
   what is mid-flight. Facts, with numbers where numbers exist.
3. **What was tried.** Including the dead ends — especially the dead ends.
   The next engineer must not re-pay for your lessons.
4. **Next steps.** Ordered, concrete, each with its first action. Flag
   which step is blocked and on whom.
5. **Risks and open questions.** What might bite, what is undecided, and
   who can decide it.

## Rules

- Write for a competent stranger, not for yourself tomorrow.
- Never hand off a claim you have not verified without labelling it
  unverified.
- A handoff is for the *engineer continuing the work*. A stakeholder
  update is a different document (see internal-comms) — do not merge them.
