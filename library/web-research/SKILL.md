# web-research

## Purpose

Research a question across multiple sources and answer it with the
receipts attached. (In this platform's offline tier the "sources" are
the seeded corpus; the procedure is identical against a live web tool
bound to the same research action.)

## Procedure

1. **Decompose the question** into checkable claims before searching.
2. **Gather from at least two independent sources** per load-bearing
   claim. One source is a lead, not a finding.
3. **Cross-check.** Where sources disagree, report the disagreement —
   the answer is the disagreement, priced.
4. **Cite every load-bearing fact** with its source id, inline.
5. **Include counter-evidence and limits:** what you searched that
   contradicted or failed to support the answer, and what you could
   not check. A research answer without this section is advocacy.

## Rules

- Recency and authority beat volume: ten copies of one claim are one
  source.
- Never launder a source's uncertainty into your certainty.
